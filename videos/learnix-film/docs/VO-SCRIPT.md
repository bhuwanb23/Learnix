### Why this was trimmed

The first pass ran at 835 words and 96% of the film carried narration. Acts 1 and 3 were unusable: Act 3 needed **210 wpm** to fit, which is not narration, it is a read-along. Both are now inside normal speech range. **265 words were cut.** Everything below is verified against its scene duration.

Do not add copy back into Act 3. If a line feels thin, the answer is a longer scene, not a longer sentence.

## Session notes for the director

1. **Record Act 1 and Act 3 first.** They are the two acts that were rebuilt and they are the two that prove the read. Everything else falls out.
2. **S21 is the hardest cue in the film.** The bridge must land. Rehearse it cold five times before recording. If the narrator still cannot hold it, let S22 start late and shorten S22 — do not rush S21's second sentence.
3. **Act 10 runs hot by design.** If S55–S58 feel breathless in playback, the fix is a 2s extension on S56, not a cut to the copy.
4. **Do not over-act S47.** *"And records that it did"* is the most important phrase in the intelligence act and the easiest to oversell. Understate it.
5. **No music under S21's first sentence.** The beat needs silence around it.
6. **S27 must be re-recorded if the ring changes.** It is a verbatim read of the eight module names.
