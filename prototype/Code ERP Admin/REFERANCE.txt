
## 1️⃣ Administration & Finance – Admin Side (ERP) – Single Page Dashboard

**Objective:**
Design an **All-in-One Admin Dashboard** for the *Administration & Finance module* of a College ERP system. The focus is on helping Finance Officers, Account Managers, and Administrators **track budgets, manage workflows, and streamline approvals**, with **all key data visible on a single page** for quick actions.

### Core Functional Panels

1. **AI-driven Budget Forecasting**

   * Predict upcoming **expenses and income trends** using historical data.
   * Show **budget allocation vs. actual spending** in summary cards.
   * Provide alerts for **budget overruns** or **unused funds**.

2. **Auto-Scholarship Eligibility**

   * Automatically detect students who qualify for scholarships based on **academic performance and financial data**.
   * Display eligible student lists with **approval actions**.
   * Track scholarship disbursement history.

3. **Digital Approvals Workflow**

   * Manage **leave requests, expense claims, and circular approvals**.
   * Timeline view showing pending, approved, and rejected tasks.
   * Quick approval/rejection buttons for faster decisions.

4. **Smart Notifications**

   * Fee reminders for students.
   * Alerts for pending approvals.
   * Auto-generated notifications for important deadlines.

5. **Reports Panel**

   * Monthly and yearly financial summaries.
   * Exportable reports for audits and compliance.

## 2️⃣ Academics & Examinations – Admin Side (ERP) – Single Page Dashboard

**Objective:**
Provide an **integrated academic and exam monitoring dashboard** for faculty, exam controllers, and administrators to **automate scheduling, evaluations, and student performance analysis**.

### Core Functional Panels

1. **Auto Exam Timetable Generator**

   * Auto-create timetables avoiding class/subject clashes.
   * Display **exam schedule cards** with room and invigilator details.
   * Highlight overlapping subjects or conflicts.

2. **Digital Evaluation Dashboard**

   * Track percentage of answer sheets evaluated.
   * Show progress bars for subjects and examiners.
   * Flag subjects pending completion.

3. **AI Cheating Detection**

   * Monitor **online MCQ tests** for suspicious activity.
   * Generate flagged reports for admins.
   * Integration with proctoring logs.

4. **Student Academic Heatmap**

   * Visual heatmap of weak and strong subjects.
   * Student-level and batch-level performance breakdown.
   * Identify at-risk students for interventions.

5. **Reports Panel**

   * Exam results analytics (pass %, top scorers, subject trends).
   * Export options for academic councils.

**Objective:**
Provide Student Affairs Officers and Welfare Admins with tools to **manage grievances, hostel attendance, transport, and health records** in one unified dashboard.

### Core Functional Panels

1. **Anonymous Grievance Box**

   * Ticket-based grievance submission.
   * Resolution tracker with status: Pending, In-Progress, Resolved.
   * Analytics of most frequent complaint categories.

2. **Hostel Smart Attendance**

   * QR code or Face-ID based entry system.
   * Real-time hostel occupancy status.
   * Logs for late entries or absentees.

3. **Bus GPS Tracking**

   * Interactive live map with all buses.
   * Delay and breakdown alerts.
   * Clickable bus cards with driver details.

4. **Health Dashboard**

   * Vaccination status of students and staff.
   * Medical visits and health check-up records.
   * Alerts for health compliance requirements.

5. **Reports Panel**

   * Monthly summaries for grievances, attendance, and health.

## 7️⃣ Infrastructure & Estate – Admin Side (ERP) – Single Page Dashboard

**Objective:**
Help Estate Officers, Facility Managers, and Library Admins **monitor, manage, and maintain campus infrastructure efficiently** from one consolidated dashboard.

### Core Functional Panels

1. **Maintenance Requests Panel**

   * View and assign repair tickets for classrooms, labs, hostels, and utilities.
   * Track by status: Pending, In-Progress, Completed.
   * Prioritize urgent issues like power failures or equipment breakdowns.

2. **Library Analytics Console**

   * Track most-used books, journals, and e-resources.
   * Auto-send overdue reminders.
   * Suggest new purchases based on trends.

3. **IoT Monitoring Dashboard**

   * Real-time monitoring of electricity and water usage.
   * Track lab equipment uptime/downtime.
   * Monthly efficiency reports.

4. **Security Management**

   * Visitor logs with approval system.
   * CCTV alerts and incident tracking.
   * Emergency notification management.

5. **Asset Records**

   * Inventory of classrooms, labs, hostels, furniture.
   * Depreciation and maintenance schedules.
   * Exportable audit-ready reports.
