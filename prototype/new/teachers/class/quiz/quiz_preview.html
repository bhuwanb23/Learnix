QUESTION 03 5.0 POINTS

Explain the role of NMDA receptors as coincidence detectors.

Student text area preview...

auto_awesome Teacher Reference Key

The NMDA receptor requires both glutamate binding (presynaptic activity) and postsynaptic depolarization to remove the magnesium block (Mg2+), thus acting as a molecular coincidence detector for LTP.
