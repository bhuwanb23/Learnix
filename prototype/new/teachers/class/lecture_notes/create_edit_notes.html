The shift from Theocentric (God-centered) to Anthropocentric (Human-centered) worldviews defined the Italian Renaissance.

Key themes to cover in this lecture:

  • The discovery of linear perspective by Brunelleschi.
  • The patronage of the Medici family in Florence.
  • Humanism as an intellectual bridge to Classical antiquity.

Next week, we will examine how these concepts traveled North to the Netherlands and Germany.
